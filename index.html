Ask us a question
How do you approach patient safety?

Your procedure is discussed with the medical team, who assess your individual health and suitability. The surgeon and clinic determine the appropriate care plan. We help coordinate logistics, but do not replace medical advice or clinical care.

How long should I plan to stay in Tunisia?

It depends on your procedure, your recovery, and your surgeon’s recommendations. Your medical team will advise on a safe timeline before you make travel plans.

What post-operative support is available?

GlowLink can coordinate personalized local assistance, follow-up, and—in the VIP package—an optional private nurse at your accommodation, subject to availability and your care plan.

Are the hotel partners confirmed?

Accommodation arrangements are being finalized. We’ll share suitable 4- and 5-star options in the Tunis lake district and northern suburbs when we discuss your dates.
