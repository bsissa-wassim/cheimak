Good to know

A few things you may be wondering.

Ask us a question
Who decides which dental care is appropriate?

The dental team evaluates your situation and explains the care options. GlowLink helps coordinate the practical journey; clinical decisions remain with the treating professionals.

How long should I plan to stay?

The duration depends on the care discussed with your dental team and the support you need. Confirm the proposed schedule before arranging travel.

Can a loved one take part in the planning?

Yes, you can include someone you trust in the conversation. Tell us how you would like them involved and whether they will accompany you.

How is senior support organized?

We begin by listening to your needs, preferences and daily rhythm. The support to consider, its availability and the practical arrangements are discussed before confirmation.
